# First Person Cameras

This mod adds natural head and body movement to the first person view, both on foot and in the cab.

- On foot, the view moves with your footsteps, sways slightly and breathes, and dips when you land from a jump.
- In the cab, you feel the seat suspension over bumps, lean into corners, nod under braking, and get a bit of engine rumble through the seat.
- The motion is measured from what the vehicle is actually doing, so it works on every vehicle, including modded ones.
- Every effect can be tuned or turned off in the settings menu, and each player's settings are their own in multiplayer.
- Right Ctrl + B toggles the on-foot effects, and Right Ctrl + N toggles the in-cab effects.

It is purely cosmetic: nothing is saved into your savegame and nothing is sent over the network.
